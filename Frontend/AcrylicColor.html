Acrylic Color

Course Description

Explore the versatility and vibrancy of acrylic paints. This course is designed to help you master this fast-drying medium, covering techniques from blending and glazing to texture building and pouring. Acrylics offer the brilliance of oils with the convenience of water-based cleanup.

Whether you want to create bold contemporary pieces or realistic studies, this course provides the tools and techniques to express your creativity.
