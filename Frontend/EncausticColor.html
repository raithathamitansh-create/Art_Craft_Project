Encaustic Color

Course Description

Experience the ancient and luminous art of encaustic painting. This unique medium uses heated beeswax mixed with pigments to create textured, multi-layered works of art. You will learn safety protocols, melting and mixing wax, and techniques for fusing, scraping, and embedding.

Discover the incredible depth and surface qualities that only wax can provide. Ideal for artists looking to experiment with texture and mixed media.
