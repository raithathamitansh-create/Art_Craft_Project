Water Color

Course Description

Dive into the fluid and transparent world of watercolors. This course covers everything from basic washes and layering to advanced dry brush and wet-on-wet techniques. Discover how to capture light, atmosphere, and delicate textures in your artwork.

Learn to control water and pigment to create stunning landscapes, florals, and portraits. Perfect for artists who enjoy a spontaneous and expressive medium.
