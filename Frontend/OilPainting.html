Oil Painting

Course Description

Master the timeless art of oil painting. This course takes you from the basics of color mixing and brushwork to advanced techniques like glazing, impasto, and chiaroscuro. Whether you are a beginner or looking to refine your skills, you will learn how to create rich, vibrant, and expressive oil paintings.

Our expert instructors will guide you through the process of choosing materials, preparing your canvas, and building your composition step-by-step.
